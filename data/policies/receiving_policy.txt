Receiving and Inventory Policy

Section 1.1 — Delivery Verification

Warehouse staff must compare the physical quantity
received against the corresponding purchase order.

Section 1.2 — Short Deliveries

If received quantity is lower than ordered quantity,
staff must record the discrepancy and confirm whether
the remaining quantity is scheduled for another delivery.

Section 1.3 — Inventory Records

Inventory records must reflect verified physical
receipts. Adjustments require approval from an
authorized inventory supervisor.
