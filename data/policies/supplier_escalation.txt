Supplier Exception Escalation Policy

Section 3.1 — Supplier Communication

Purchasing staff should contact the supplier when a
delivery discrepancy is identified.

Section 3.2 — Supporting Evidence

Staff should request shipment confirmation, packing
slips, or a delivery schedule for missing quantities.

Section 3.3 — Escalation

Unresolved discrepancies must be referred to an
authorized procurement manager. Supplier penalties
must not be imposed automatically.
