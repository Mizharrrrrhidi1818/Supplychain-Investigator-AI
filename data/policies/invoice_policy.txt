
SUPPLYCHAIN-AI DEMO COMPANY
Invoice Verification Policy

Section 2.1 — Three-Way Matching

Purchase orders, delivery records, and supplier invoices
must be compared before invoice approval.

Section 2.2 — Quantity Mismatches

If invoiced quantity exceeds received quantity,
accounts payable must flag the invoice for human review
and request supporting delivery documentation.

Section 2.3 — Payment Decisions

The authorized finance employee must decide whether to
hold, partially approve, or otherwise resolve an invoice.
An AI system must not change payment status automatically.
